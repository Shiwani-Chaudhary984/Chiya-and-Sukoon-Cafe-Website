Our Story:
Finding Sukoon

In Nepali culture, *Chiya* is more than just a cup of spiced tea—it is a warm embrace, an unhurried invitation to pause and connect. *Sukoon* represents the deep, soothing calm that follows.

We designed Chiya and Sukoon to be your neighborhood sanctuary. Surrounded by sunlit floral archways, earthy terracotta tones, and the fragrance of cardamom and roasted beans, you are always welcome to stay as long as you wish.

Our Roots & Heritage

The Art of Slow Living

Founded with a deep appreciation for Himalayan hospitality, Chiya and Sukoon is an invitation to slow down, share stories, and rediscover inner calm.

What is Sukoon?

Sukoon translates to peace of mind, serenity, and complete solace. In a world of rushing deadlines and constant digital noise, we wanted to build a sanctuary where you can hear yourself think.

Our tea leaves come straight from high-altitude estates in Nepal and Darjeeling. Simmered slowly with freshly ground spices in small batches, every sip carries warmth, richness, and tradition.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you accommodate vegan and dairy-free diets?

Yes! All of our spiced Chiya blends can be prepared with organic oat milk or almond milk, and we have a dedicated selection of vegan momos and bakery items.

Is there high-speed Wi-Fi and quiet workspaces?

Absolutely. Our Hearth room and side lounges are equipped with high-speed fiber internet and charging ports at every booth.

Do you accept walk-ins for table seating?

We always reserve 40% of our seating for walk-in guests. For guaranteed sunroom seating or groups larger than 4, reservations are recommended.
